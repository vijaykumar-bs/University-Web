Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC)

Ensuring a culture of quality and continuous improvement in all academic and administrative activities at ABC University.

Quality Policy

ABC University is committed to establishing a quality system that ensures the highest standards in higher education and research.

The IQAC works towards:

  • Developing benchmarks for academic and administrative activities.
  • Facilitating a learner-centric environment conducive to quality education.
  • Arranging for feedback responses from students, parents, and other stakeholders.
  • Organizing inter and intra-institutional workshops and seminars on quality-related themes.
